# P68 Ketch — Proposed Open-Source Design Release Policy

**Scale:** 120.0 meters LOA / approximately 394 feet. This is not a 68-foot amateur kit boat.

## Release classes

1. **Concept / educational:** parametric concept geometry, preliminary drawings, hydrostatic assumptions, diagrams, and presentation material. This material is NOT approved for construction.
2. **Engineering-reviewed fabrication package (future):** material specifications, approved scantlings, plate developments, frame parts, welding/inspection requirements, CNC nesting layouts with kerf and forming allowances, signed revision records, and proof of appropriate design authority. Release only when such work actually exists and the authors/rightsholders approve distribution.
3. **As-built and trials (future):** inspection records, deviations and redlines, stability tests, sea trials, and change logs. Records are design-specific, not blanket certification for third-party builds.

The project must resolve license ownership before promising distribution. Suggested repository structure: `design/`, `hydrostatics/`, `ga/`, `structures/`, `fabrication/`, `as-built/`, `verification/`, `licenses/` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

Open licenses do not make a design structurally sufficient, certified, or fit for a particular vessel. New builders remain responsible for engineering, applicable approvals, yard capability, and legal compliance.

## Crowdfunding transparency

Disclose plainly that funds may support design development and a privately owned yacht; distinguish public outcomes from private property ownership. Publish defined milestone budgets, release conditions, postponement rules, progress reports, intellectual-property status, and refund or fulfillment terms. Do not represent donors as owners, investors, or guaranteed recipients of returns without proper legal structure and regulatory review.

## Production gate

No nesting files may be described as cutting-ready until a competent marine engineer/naval architect, appropriate classification reviewer (where applicable), and yard production engineering have verified the hull design, material grades, forming allowances, fatigue and weld details, and dimensional control plan.
