# Final hull size: 66.0 m LOA, 60.2 m LWL, 12.1 m beam

Status: final size for the design, derived from the room-fit minimum, the buoyancy relation, and the lessons from the earlier passes. The remaining checks (stability, lines plan, mass) are listed at the end and are not closed by this note.

## Final values

| Dimension | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| LOA | **66.0 m** | 64.0 m room-fit minimum, plus a 2.0 m end allowance |
| LWL | **60.2 m** | Waterline ratio 0.912 kept from the earlier design |
| Beam | **12.1 m** | Kept. Stability and ballast set it, to be confirmed |
| L/B | 5.45 | Within the range for a blue-water ketch |

## Derivation

1. **Room-fit minimum: 64.0 m.** The smallest LOA at which all room corners lie inside the ellipse outline at 12.1 m beam. This has no margin, so it is a floor, not a design length.
2. **End allowance: +2.0 m, to 66.0 m.** The lessons from the previous pass: the ellipse is idealised, and a real hull needs structure at the stem and transom, plus space for the mizzen at the stern and the bowsprit or fittings at the bow. A 1.0 m allowance at each end is a design assumption, and it is the value adopted here. Any different allowance is a change to this derivation.
3. **Beam: 12.1 m, kept.** Rooms need about 10 m, but stability and ballast need the beam, and the beam was not derived from those. It stays at the earlier value until a stability study says otherwise.
4. **Waterline: 60.2 m.** The recorded LWL/LOA ratio of 0.912, applied to 66.0 m.

## Buoyancy at the final size

Displacement is Δ = ρ × Cb × LWL × B × T, with ρ = 1.025 t/m³ and Cb = 0.375 (midpoint of the placeholder range 0.30 to 0.45). With LWL × B = 728.4 m²:

| Draft | Displacement |
|---|---|
| Keel-up 4.8 m | **1,344 t** |
| Keel-down 7.2 m | **2,016 t** |

At the earlier reference of 806 t, the draft would be 2.88 m, which is below the keel-up target.

**Design decision:** the design displacement target is **about 1,350 t**, sized from the keel-up draft at this hull. The earlier reference of 806 t is replaced by that target. The keel-up and keel-down targets stand. The lessons from the earlier passes make this the correct direction: the keel draft is a fixed requirement, and the displacement must follow it.

## Area budget at the final size

Gross deck area is π/4 × 66.0 × 12.1 = 627.2 m². The area left after the requests and the 30% allowance:

| Deck | Left (m²) |
|---|---|
| Bridge | 304.1 |
| Commander / Lido | 228.8 |
| Main / Master | 206.0 |
| Tween | 133.8 |
| Tank / Technical | 165.5 |

Every level keeps room for future change. Tween is the tightest.

## Lessons from the earlier passes that shaped this size

- **One outline for every deck overstated the volume.** The final size keeps the ellipse only as a plan-area and room-fit model. The per-deck outlines must narrow with height before any volume figure is used.
- **Room positions set the length, not the area.** The minimum length came from the forward rooms, so the length follows the layout, not the budget.
- **The draft targets did not match the reference displacement.** The displacement target now follows the draft.
- **The idealised outline has no margin.** The end allowance is added for that reason.

## Still to be considered

These are not closed by this note, and each can move the size or the displacement:

1. **Stability.** GM and the centre of gravity, with the tall ketch rig, the tenders, the batteries and the steel structure. Beam is only justified once this is done.
2. **Lines plan.** Real block and waterplane coefficients, and real end shapes. The placeholder Cb of 0.375 must be replaced.
3. **Mass.** The weight register must reach about 1,350 t, with every item carried. It is 0% covered at the time of writing. Steel structure, batteries, tenders and stainless fittings are the largest items.
4. **Freeboard.** Not calculated here. The deck heights and the sheer must be checked against it.
5. **Rig.** Mast heights and positions, the stays and their load paths, and the mizzen at the stern.
6. **Per-deck outlines** at 66 m, generated so that the layout and access checks run against the final hull.
7. **Margin.** Whether 1.0 m per end is enough for the real stem and transom structure.
