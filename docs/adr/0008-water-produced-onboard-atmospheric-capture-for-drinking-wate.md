# ADR-0008: Water: produced onboard; atmospheric capture for drinking water only

Status: Accepted (owner for production; sizing proposed)

## Context

Water is produced onboard. Reverse osmosis carries demand, and atmospheric capture covers drinking water only. Water embarked is a storage buffer, not provisioning.

## Decision

Atmospheric units sized to drinking water: 5 to 13 units normal, 36 on event days. Buffer sized for a placeholder 72-hour outage.

## Open gates

- Occupancy figures (crew, guests, pets, event visitors)
- Outage period for the buffer and drinking-only or full-demand coverage
- Potable treatment and remineralisation
