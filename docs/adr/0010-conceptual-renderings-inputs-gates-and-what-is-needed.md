# ADR-0010: Conceptual renderings: inputs, gates and what is needed

Status: Proposed

## Context

The owner wants conceptual renderings of the new features. This session cannot generate photoreal images. Schematic diagrams are produced from the repo data instead.

## Decision

Renderings are produced only from approved geometry and approved materials. Mood images follow the materials policy and the interior aesthetic deferral.

## Open gates

- Lines plan and dimensioned general arrangement (for geometry)
- Materials specification approved for interior and exterior (for finishes)
- Interior aesthetic decision (deferred, docs/materials-policy.md)
- Render brief updated to the materials policy (current renders show silk and leather)
- Image-generation tool available to the session (not available in this session)
