# ADR-0004: Energy storage: rackmount LFP buffer, option B

Status: Proposed (recommended for review)

## Context

The battery is a buffer for peaks and quiet running. Endurance comes from fuel and generation, not from batteries. Battery-only endurance does not close at any placeholder load.

## Decision

Rackmount LFP, about 400 kWh usable, eight racks spread with air gaps (option B). Battery room sealed to IP67 with closed-loop cooling.

## Open gates

- Module size (6 kWh needed for eight racks)
- Battery room floor (9.6 m² placeholder) and separation from fuel
- Fire-safety review: pressure relief sizing and gas-tight exhaust
