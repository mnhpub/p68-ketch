# ADR-0006: Materials policy: no foam, teak outdoors, stainless for rigid appointments

Status: Accepted (owner)

## Context

No foam anywhere on the craft. Teak only for outdoor decking. No synthetics in outdoor accommodation, with a controlled sealant exception. Stainless steel for rigid appointments. Wood non-structural. Silk, gold, silver, ivory and exotic materials excluded.

## Decision

Full rules in docs/materials-policy.md. Non-foam insulation required for walk-in and cellar panels.

## Open gates

- Whether mineral wool counts as synthetic
- Whether wood joinery is a rigid appointment
- Whether teak is exotic under the ban
- Coating system for structural steel (no exposed steel rule)
